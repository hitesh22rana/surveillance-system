What arming does

It keeps a snapshot for you to look at. It does not say who it was, which way they came, or what they meant - the camera reports one class, person, so it cannot tell family from anyone else.

Camera 4 is always armed and has no control. Home and Away do not touch it, and nothing you switch changes it. On Cameras 1–3, Home keeps nothing and Away keeps every person in view; switch one yourself and the mode reads Custom.

Turning review off from Away is confirmed. One camera or the whole set, the page asks first. Cancel or Escape sends nothing and changes nothing.

Your choice is saved, so it survives a restart, and a fresh install starts at Home. Arming a camera only takes effect while its area is confirmed by the camera service; if that cannot be read the camera keeps your setting and keeps nothing until it can be read again. Switching off stops further snapshots being kept and does not release any that are already kept. Snapshots saved before any of this are left exactly as they were.

On Cameras 1–3 an entry that had already started when you armed can be missed, because it has one start time for the whole time somebody was on camera. Camera 4 has no arming moment, so it keeps those too. There is no fighting or behaviour detection here at all.

Saved detections

An ordinary detection in a monitored area expires after 6 h. A person inside a watched area while that camera is armed - and always on Camera 4 - is held until you delete it.

How the row labels are worked out

6-hour retention expires on its own. Restricted area entry means a person was inside that camera’s own restricted area - a candidate for review, nothing more. Kept for review is held until you delete it: a person inside a watched area while that camera is armed, or an animal on sight on the first three cameras. Previously kept was held before this rule existed and is left exactly as it was. The Kept for review filter is the camera service’s own kept flag and nothing else, so it includes the Previously kept rows too. In this prototype a monitored area is the whole camera frame.
