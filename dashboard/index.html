How the row labels are worked out

6-hour retention expires on its own. Loitering ≥60 s is the camera service counting how long it tracked one person inside a monitored area. Kept for review is held until you delete it. A person gets here by loitering; an animal gets here on sight, on the first three cameras. Previously kept was held from before the 60 second rule existed. In this prototype a monitored area is the whole camera frame.
