How these badges and the 60 seconds are worked out

6-hour retention expires on its own · Loitering ≥60 s is the camera service’s own tally of frames it tracked inside a monitored area, so it is not a claim that anyone stood still · Kept for review is held until you delete it · Previously kept is held from before the 60 s rule existed. In this prototype the monitored area is the full camera frame.
