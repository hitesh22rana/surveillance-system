Entry monitoring

A new person entering the monitored area 1 on Camera 1, or the area_4 on Camera 4, is kept for review while you have that camera armed.

Camera 4 entry · reading status

What arming does

It keeps a snapshot for you to look at. It does not say who it was, which way they came, or what they meant — the camera reports one class, person, so it cannot tell family from anyone else.

Restarting the service disarms both cameras. Arm again after a restart if you want it. Turning it off stops further snapshots being kept and does not release any that are already kept. Snapshots saved before any of this are left exactly as they were.

An entry that had already started when you armed can be missed, because it has one start time for the whole time somebody was on camera. There is no fighting or behaviour detection here at all, and Camera 2 and Camera 3 are not watched for anything like it.

Saved detections

An ordinary detection in a monitored area expires after 6 h. A person entering a restricted area on Camera 1 or Camera 4 while you have armed it is held until you delete it.

How the row labels are worked out

6-hour retention expires on its own. Restricted area entry means a person was inside that camera’s own restricted area — a candidate for review, nothing more. Kept for review is held until you delete it: a person gets here through an armed restricted area, an animal on sight on the first three cameras. Previously kept was held before this rule existed and is left exactly as it was. In this prototype a monitored area is the whole camera frame.
